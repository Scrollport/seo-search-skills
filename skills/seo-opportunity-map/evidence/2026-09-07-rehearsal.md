# Organic Opportunity Map 3.0.0 — internal verification

7 September 2026. Verified for the bounded research outcome; not Customer-proven. No client endorsement, site changes, deployment or publication occurred.

## Scope and result

A public UK trade-business brief produced two existing-page improvements and one product-fit hold, with buyer task, current page ownership, source evidence, owner roles and completion checks. It did not promise traffic or manufacture a third recommendation. The client has not reviewed usefulness or impact.

- Six 100-row domain samples across a target and two competitors: the initial default sample was poorly suited to opportunity discovery; one volume-ordered set improved coverage. Preserve both and charge both. Runtime now selects ordering for the question before sampling.
- Two keyword-overview batches, five current SERPs/ownership searches and selected page checks supported the decisions.
- The first extractor omitted main category content. A conditional second extractor recovered it and prevented recommending information already present. This is a completeness check, not a mandatory provider count.
- Related phrase volumes were not summed; a missing returned phrase remained unavailable. Trade and consumer audiences were distinguished.

SEO-labelled settled cost: **$0.290400**. Two relevant pages from the companion AI review were reused; the combined three-Skill run total, charged once per call, was **$0.655100**, below the user's $5 maximum. No outstanding holds.

## Quality basis and limits

The revision uses original wording informed by the business/page ownership approach in [seo-plan](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-seo/blob/a1480c7e590b16001bd9dc1627eacdcd44d580f9/skills/seo-plan/SKILL.md) and evidence limitations in [seo-audit](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/blob/5b2c0007766c6a1cf1d53fd8fc73e979e0821022/skills/seo-audit/SKILL.md). These repositories exceeded 10,000 stars at review; popularity is not an output-quality measurement. A later unblinded offline comparison applied the reference Skills to the same frozen evidence and reached broadly similar practical decisions. It does not establish independent collection performance or general superiority.

Unrelated static scenarios covered SaaS, ecommerce and local services, including supplied first-party data and complete page extraction. No test business, domain, industry-specific edit or fixed success count is embedded in runtime instructions. A second unrelated live business and external review remain needed to establish broader reliability and customer value.

An independent offline agent then used a fictional Canadian software brief without expected answers. It produced two justified existing-page edits, retained a useful branded commercial query and rejected incomplete-extraction claims. Resulting clarifications let supplied evidence and caller scope override collection/count defaults and disclose missing upstream receipts. This is a behaviour check, not additional live or customer proof.
